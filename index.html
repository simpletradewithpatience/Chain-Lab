Chain Lab

Educational tool. STWP Chain Lab computes analytics from the option-chain file you upload. It does not give buy, sell or hold recommendations, and nothing here is investment advice. Options carry a high risk of loss; as per SEBI, 9 out of 10 individual traders in equity F&O incurred net losses.

Method: forward from put-call parity (median of the 5 strikes where call and put prices are closest), Black-76 with zero interest rate for IV and Greeks, lognormal distribution at ATM IV for Probability of Profit. Expiry is taken at 15:30 IST.
